# Viral Patterns — @realvladd
_Last updated: 2026-08-01 (corpus: ~40 posts/threads, full profile)_

Median likes ≈ 2. Hit = >6 likes (3× median). Top hit = 866 (SpaceX IPO).

## Hits (what works)
- **Money/access unlock for regular people** — SpaceX IPO via Fidelity, $500K→$2K (866 likes, 147 replies). 400× the account median. Two sentences, hard numbers, zero AI jargon, zero bullets. The only post that escaped the AI niche.
- **Ecosystem drama / platform politics** — "Notion just disabled all Claude models" (31), "Cursor sold to SpaceX" framing. Conflict between named companies + a takeaway lesson ("route, don't marry").
- **Job-fear with a twist** — "every chemistry guy is cooked" (~26 likes, ~44 replies — reply magnet). Formula: [profession] + concrete AI result + softening punchline ("just the boring half").
- **Insider/leak framing** — "Claude Mythos 5 slug spotted" (9/8), "leaked audio", "Polymarket quietly killed", "Anthropic quietly". The word "quietly" and BREAKING framing signal exclusive info.
- **Confession + numbers** — "backtest +180%, live −12%" (21/5). Public failure with exact numbers beats success stories.
- **Contrarian engineering take with a one-liner** — "Stop writing 800-line system prompts" (39), "loop engineering is the boss level" (25/12), "the harness is the rest of the car" (10/5). Attack a common practice, end with a quotable law.
- **Show-the-tool video** — "ant" terminal video (~87/20/4): a demo of something devs can run beats any text about it.

## Duds (what doesn't)
- **Product feature recaps** — Opus 4.8 launch (2), Fable 5 launch (2), Claude Science (2), prompting-guide summary (5). Even perfect formatting dies without conflict or personal stake. This is 80% of the account's volume and ~5% of its engagement.
- **Personal Polymarket betting diary** — consistently 1 like (World Cup, BTC predictions, verdict recaps, journey posts). Wrong audience on Threads.
- **Multi-part threads** — engagement collapses after part 1 every time (5→0, 21→0, 4→1). One self-contained post > thread.
- **Posting into communities** (AI Threads, Polymarket tags) — no measurable boost.

## Post formula
1. **Hook line:** a concrete number or named-company conflict in sentence one. No "So I was thinking…". Best hooks: "X just did Y" / "$A → $B" / "[group] is cooked".
2. **Body:** either 2-3 punchy sentences (SpaceX style) or ≤6 "→" bullets, each a hard fact. Never both long.
3. **Length:** ≤500 chars. Single post, no threads.
4. **Punchline:** one quotable law/stake for the reader ("Frontier access is now conditional", "route, don't marry").
5. **No hashtags.** Lowercase-casual voice is fine and on-brand.

## Magnet topics (ranked)
1. Money/access unlocks for regular people (IPO minimums, price drops, free tiers, new ways to earn)
2. AI job displacement — specific professions, specific numbers
3. Big-tech conflict/drama (acquisitions, providers dropping each other, lawsuits, bans)
4. Leaks & quiet changes (unannounced features, spotted model slugs, silent policy shifts)
5. Contrarian takes on dev practice (with a named alternative)
6. Wild benchmark/usage numbers — only when framed as stakes, not specs

## Scoring rubric (0-10)
+3 money/access angle for regular people
+2 hard numbers in the hook line
+2 conflict/stakes between named actors
+1 urgency/deadline ("ends tomorrow", "this week")
+1 "regular people" relevance beyond devs
+1 contrarian take / breaks an assumption
−3 reads like a press-release recap
−2 no numbers anywhere
−2 requires a thread to make sense

## Notes for generation
- The account's ceiling is proven by ONE general-audience money post. Aim ≥1 of every wave at magnet topic #1-2 (broad), ≤1 at #3-5 (niche dev audience).
- Replies come from job-fear and questions; likes come from money/drama. Both count for growth.
- X CTA in ~every 3rd post max ("full breakdown on my X → x.com/dayvanxd").
